# book-figure-session-outcomes-k115

## Goal

Port the first UML state view, building the small state-machine module the
second state view will reuse, and report its layout cost.

## Context

- Figure: `session-outcomes`: 9 states (5 phase or outcome states in two
  semantic roles), 11 transitions with multi-line guard labels, two of them green,
  an initial marker, and a 10-line key. This is the densest unproven layout; two
  transitions fan out to four outcome states, and one phase has long guards
  between close nodes.
- Record design (`book-figure-layout-record-k120`, survey option B): `_state.typ` returns a layout record (plain data, no style) and the figure source passes it to `_draw.typ`'s render; nothing here draws. Each regenerated figure commits `<figure>.layout.json` beside its SVG (`book-figure-layout-json-check-k122`), and it is shipped sketched at roughness 2 with keys crisp (`book-figure-record-and-draw-k121`).
- New engine code: `_state.typ` (rounded state in a role, initial marker,
  transition with label and optional colour, label placement beside the edge).
  Decide at the start whether to hand-place coordinates or compute a simple
  layered layout from measured sizes. If hand placement proves costly, the survey's
  option E can feed the same record: ELK on Bun (elkjs, which adds a Bun stage and
  a lockfile) or Graphviz through diagraph-layout inside Typst (0.0.1, unstable),
  each given Typst-measured sizes and checked against the "no label touches a
  line" rule. Either way the output is a record; this is not a drawing decision. The sequence engine computed from text
  measurements and the C4 figures were hand-placed; pick on the evidence of which
  edges are hard.
- Preserve the "What each view means" row in `docs/book/figures.md`: cleanup is
  attempted on every path and an attempt need not succeed; the only green route
  needs a valid result, no cancellation, confirmed cleanup and a successful final
  write; the view groups operations rather than giving precedence.

## Done when

- `session-outcomes.puml` is replaced by a committed `.typ` and `.layout.json`; `_state.typ` exists.
- Layout cost recorded as numbers in the decision log, compared with the plan's
  cost table. If the cost is far above the estimate, say so and stop before the
  next state leaf rather than spending it; the owner decides.
- `task docs:figures`, `task docs:book:build` and `task check` pass.

## Decisions (running log)

- 2026-10-17 — Method chosen at the start: neither hand-placed coordinates nor a layered-layout engine (ELK, diagraph-layout), but **grid-cell placement with measured sizes**, because the figure's topology is a spine of three phases with a fan of outcomes below it, and both state views are near-ladders. A state has a `col` and a `row` (fractional allowed); the module sizes columns and rows from the measured text and routes orthogonal transitions with the C4 module's ports and routes, which moved to `_layout.typ` (as `fit` did in k114) so `_c4.typ` and `_state.typ` share them. No Bun stage, no lockfile, no new pin. The hard edges the brief named were the fan-out (solved by an "align" port, level with the target's centre and kept inside the source's side, so the four leave `settling` one at a time with no offset written) and the long guards between close nodes (solved by choosing a side for four labels and one row gap).
- 2026-10-17 — `session-outcomes` is ported to `session-outcomes.typ` (48 lines, 10 of them the key) on a new `_state.typ` (203 lines, 56 of them comments), with the regenerated `session-outcomes.svg` (184 KB) and `session-outcomes.layout.json`. `session-outcomes.puml` is removed. The engine changes are additive: `_draw.typ` gets a `circle` shape (the initial marker) and a rounded outline for a box whose corner is over 3pt (the sketched outline followed a sharp rectangle where the fill was rounded, seen in the first render); `_style.typ` gets the roles `phase`, `outcome` and `initial`; `_layout.typ` gets `circle-el` and the moved routes. The other eight SVGs and records regenerated byte-identical (`task docs:figures:update` rewrote only `session-outcomes`).
- 2026-10-17 — Layout cost, as numbers: 9 states, 1 initial marker, 11 transitions. Coordinates written: 0. Sides named by hand: 6 of 11 transitions (5 took the facing sides); `jog` 0. Label placement chosen: 4 of the 10 labelled transitions (3 `side: "left"`, and one `seg`, `side` and `t`); `dx`, `dy` and wrapping widths 0. Other numbers chosen: 1 row gap (100, below the fan, to give the long green guard room), 1 `min-w` (250, on the cleaning-up state, so the fan's lines sit far enough apart for a label between two of them). Renders to settle: 5 (the first was structurally right; the rest fixed the rounded outline in `_draw.typ`, then the green guard's place twice, then the row gap and `min-w`, then a line break I had added to the green guard). Minutes of work: not clocked; about 40 to 50, an estimate, nearly all of it the new module and the rounded outline; the reading of `_c4.typ`, `_draw.typ`, `figures.md` and the old source beforehand is not counted.
- 2026-10-17 — Was the k108 table right? It estimated **High** ("fan-out to 4 outcomes, label placement"). The actual is Medium for the module (a 203-line layout module and two small drawing additions) and Low to Medium for the figure (6 sides and 4 label placements, no coordinate, no hand route, no label nudge). The cost is **not far above the estimate**, it is below it, so this leaf does not stop before `book-figure-settled-exit-status-k116`. What the table did not see is that the fan-out was the cheap part (the "align" port); the label that cost renders was the one long green guard, which wants a vertical segment with room beside it, and a placement rule for it would be a feature (a label that avoids what it touches) the module does not have.
- 2026-10-17 — What was clumsy: (1) a label's place is a segment, a side and a fraction (`seg`, `side`, `t`), so the green guard took three numbers found by looking; (2) the grid has one gap per column and per row, so the extra room under the fan is a whole row gap of 100 that also separates the rows' other contents (nothing else there, here); (3) a state's width is its text unless `min-w` says otherwise, so a fan's port spacing follows the text of the source state; (4) "align" clamps a port to the side, so a target far to one side gets an L or a Z, not a straight line (the left and right outcomes enter by their sides, which reads well). `settled-exit-status` is a ladder of steps with an outcome beside each, so a column of states, `align` ports and the `right` side should cover it with no new module code; whether its tall outcome box (eight lines) needs a `limit` or a `min-w` is for k116 to find.
