# completion-failure-contract-k8


## Goal

Design the smallest justified corrections to the incumbent launcher's known
acquisition/failure contract gaps and establish the entry/lifetime contract for
the human's one-session native CLI consumer. Deliver a source-supported repair
contract, discriminating check plans and a prospectively priced next useful
increment before any new component comparison or the book-structure agreement.

## Done when

- Establish a local repair contract for each known gap: consumed cancellation
  survives runner wait/reap errors even without an ending; unconfirmed worker
  reap cannot admit selection or managed launch; partial acquisition supplies
  checked cleanup outcomes/diagnostics; and failed exec after terminal handoff
  attempts and reports required recovery. Explain resource/signal ownership,
  cleanup ordering, permitted result or failure, primary-error precedence and
  all required diagnostics, including when recovery itself fails. Assess the
  counterexamples against the required promises rather than assuming a particular
  patch or claiming every OS failure is covered by four cases.
- Establish the process-lifetime native launcher entry contract: exclusive
  signal/terminal authority, capture before relevant runtime changes, ignored
  and blocked signals, terminal INT, late cancellation and the final result or
  propagated-signal exit. Keep child entry transparency distinct from restoring
  a host that resumes arbitrary work. Preserve required terminal/mask cleanup
  within the launch; no concurrent embedding or persistent-runner restoration
  guarantee follows from the chosen CLI lifetime. An independent adopting
  project and observed independent-use result remain unestablished.
- Name the exact discriminating expectations and justified instruments at the
  human-agreed boundaries. For C1, plan interruption followed by observation
  and reap failure through the existing private process seam and no-ending
  front settlement. For C2, justify the smallest private evaluator teardown
  control and check that no managed job starts after unconfirmed reap. For C3,
  plan failure after directory acquisition plus cleanup failure at the owning
  boundary, preserving original failure and cleanup diagnostics. For C4, plan
  real PTY checks distinguishing failure before handoff from failed exec after
  observed handoff. Test controls must keep actual fixture processes reaped.
  This leaf neither constructs the instruments nor runs behavioural checks.
- Keep protected reference expectations outside the repair under judgment.

- Price and grow only the next precise, independently useful repair/planning

## Decisions (running log)

- Adopt the k7 requirements and existing acceptance seams without another
  interview. Keep the incumbent packages/processes and fixed product reference
  `0bdcb1f9ae24db5e9dd499308dd791744e908eb0`; comparative iterations, attempts,
  catalogue reads and target judgments remain zero. The first clock receipt is
  05:11:31 UTC on 2026-10-14, after bootstrap. Reserve the final 20 minutes of
  the D phase ceiling for closure; this session constructs/runs no behavioural
  instrument (P=E=X=0).
- Keep the completion design in the existing durable
  `docs/specs/result-job-completion.md`, with source anchors and discriminating
  check details in a separate evidence document. Update the existing result-job
  viewer rather than create a competing presentation. Current-source limits
  remain explicit until repairs and their checks supply runtime evidence.
- Cancellation consumed by supervision must cross its error boundary as
  explicit outcome information; a no-ending error cannot reconstruct it from
  an emptied latch. Retain the existing success APIs and errno classification,
  and give the existing opaque runner error a cancellation observation. Front
  joining remains front signal, runner outcome signal (success or failure),
  then late runner latch. Do not requeue the old signal into the global latch.
- Worker teardown is an admission prerequisite. Successful conversation plus
  unconfirmed reap becomes worker failure before resolution/selection; an
  existing refusal/timeout stays primary unless cancellation wins. Preserve
  captured policy diagnostics and report cleanup errors separately, without
  claiming the worker was reaped when it was not confirmed.
- Partial acquisition remains owned by its allocator until a successful
  transfer. After directory creation, every ordinary returned preparation
  failure must explicitly attempt checked cleanup and convey the original
  failure plus cleanup diagnostics. Keep session-directory identity protection
  and disarm destructor removal after a checked attempt; cleanup must not
  delete a replacement or silently retry away the first failure.
- Terminal failed-spawn recovery keeps the existing lease and original errno.
  A private CLOEXEC pipe can convey the attempted group before handoff without
  child-memory assumptions. It is a proposed local mechanism, with explicit
  no-competing-foreground-transfer/no-unrelated-group-reuse premises through
  handback; CLI lifetime alone cannot establish those premises. No unconditional
  no-theft or universal OS-cleanup guarantee is claimed. Required recovery and
  reporting remain the contract, and actual PTY behavior is unobserved here.
- The one-session CLI owns capture before runtime changes, signal/terminal
  authority, waitability before worker/job, transparent child ignores/mask,
  terminal-directed versus launcher-directed INT, late joining, cleanup and
  result-or-signal exit. Persistent/concurrent-host restoration is separate.
  The source's unchecked handler/mask operations and ignored-SIGCHLD ordering
  remain explicit conformance premises, not discharged by the four named cases.
- Spend the one picked-leaf fresh reviewer on the NEW repair/error/terminal
  contract, not prior k6 prose. Its first finding is a contract stated unclearly:
  query/handback and a post-reap numeric record need serialization/identity
  premises. Make those assumptions explicit in contract, evidence and caption;
  no library primitive or unconditional race guarantee is inferred. Its second
  is valid/actionable: Started means spawn-reported success, since pre-exec
  signal death can produce spawn-pipe EOF before exec. Correct that unsupported
  claim and add a dedicated PTY discriminator. This is a source-conclusive
  event-description correction, not a new launch protocol. Both findings are
  reconciled; no second review or unrecorded design cycle is spent.
- Grow only a C1 planning continuation before the book agreement. Its useful
  slice will carry cancellation across runner error and the front's no-ending
  settlement, with original/reap/recovery diagnostics and protected precedence.
  Correctness-phase closure charges conservative D≤40 and RC≤10 minutes total,
  including this session's closure/presentation/tree/commit, P=E=X=0. Remaining
  ceilings are D≤50 (including the reserved 20-minute phase closure), RC≤20,
  P≤240, E≤120, X=0. Allocate the next planning leaf D≤10/RC≤5, P=E=X=0;
  prospectively earmark P≤45/E≤25 for its subsequent C1 implementation/check
  slice, to be committed into that mandate before construction/execution. The
  earmark is not spend or permission to consume the other gaps' remainder.
