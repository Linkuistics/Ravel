# completion-failure-contract-k8


## Goal

Design the smallest justified corrections to the incumbent launcher's known
acquisition/failure contract gaps and establish the entry/lifetime contract for
the human's one-session native CLI consumer. Deliver a source-supported repair
contract, discriminating check plans and a prospectively priced next useful
increment before any new component comparison or the book-structure agreement.

## Done when

- Establish a local repair contract for each known gap: consumed cancellation
  survives runner wait/reap errors even without an ending; unconfirmed worker
  reap cannot admit selection or managed launch; partial acquisition supplies
  checked cleanup outcomes/diagnostics; and failed exec after terminal handoff
  attempts and reports required recovery. Explain resource/signal ownership,
  cleanup ordering, permitted result or failure, primary-error precedence and
  all required diagnostics, including when recovery itself fails. Assess the
  counterexamples against the required promises rather than assuming a particular
  patch or claiming every OS failure is covered by four cases.
- Establish the process-lifetime native launcher entry contract: exclusive
  signal/terminal authority, capture before relevant runtime changes, ignored
  and blocked signals, terminal INT, late cancellation and the final result or
  propagated-signal exit. Keep child entry transparency distinct from restoring
  a host that resumes arbitrary work. Preserve required terminal/mask cleanup
  within the launch; no concurrent embedding or persistent-runner restoration
  guarantee follows from the chosen CLI lifetime. An independent adopting
  project and observed independent-use result remain unestablished.
- Name the exact discriminating expectations and justified instruments at the
  human-agreed boundaries. For C1, plan interruption followed by observation
  and reap failure through the existing private process seam and no-ending
  front settlement. For C2, justify the smallest private evaluator teardown
  control and check that no managed job starts after unconfirmed reap. For C3,
  plan failure after directory acquisition plus cleanup failure at the owning
  boundary, preserving original failure and cleanup diagnostics. For C4, plan
  real PTY checks distinguishing failure before handoff from failed exec after
  observed handoff. Test controls must keep actual fixture processes reaped.
  This leaf neither constructs the instruments nor runs behavioural checks.
- Keep protected reference expectations outside the repair under judgment.
  Preserve unchanged TypeScript policy/SDK/settings/discovery and CLI status,
  exact UTF-8 result bytes, first-writer publication, nesting, cancellation and
  combined-failure precedence/diagnostics, late-signal joining, descendant/group
  cleanup, confirmed reap, terminal recovery and the final CLI write boundary.
  Record any specification/reference mismatch explicitly; do not normalise
  errors/timing or edit expectations to bless a faulty path. Document the
  corrected known failure behavior and checks still owed without asserting
  runtime conformance from source or design.
- Price and grow only the next precise, independently useful repair/planning
  increment supported by the contract and the remaining phase caps. Name its
  consumers, ownership, preserved promises, enabling points, checks, useful
  stopping state and remaining obligations. Allocate P/E before any new seam
  is constructed or executed. Place the continuation before `book-structure-k5`
  while consequential contract uncertainty remains. No speculative full repair
  tree, package extraction, comparative target or migration is selected here.
- Carry G2 six-month support/upkeep and route cost, G3 delivery/use beyond entry,
  G4 unread alternatives, G5 unexecuted runtime domains and R-W compatible-runtime
  and new-lifecycle debt. These correctness repairs alone do not establish a
  simplification gain or root completion. Preserve root independent-capability
  delivery, unchanged configuration/complete sessions, human book structure and
  the practical post-proof reader checkpoint.

## Notes

Prospective phase caps agreed in k7 and the root brief: D≤90 minutes total for
source/design/planning, including a 20-minute closure reserve; RC≤30 minutes
total for fresh review/coordination of newly produced artifacts; P≤240 minutes
total for repair/instrument/test construction; E≤120 minutes total for
discriminating and regression execution; X=0, zero production disruption.
These are ceilings, not a guarantee of closure. This leaf begins the phase,
with no previous correctness-phase charges; k4/k6 history is distinct.

This leaf is design-only: D≤90, RC≤30, P=E=X=0, one session. At most one fresh
reviewer may inspect the new repair contract if earned under Grove's picked-leaf
rules; if review is scheduled as a tree leaf, it owns the route. Do not spend
that allowance re-reviewing unchanged k6 prose. Source/check planning and
presentation verification spend D; adding an executable instrument or observing
behaviour would spend P/E and is not this leaf's charter.

New comparative iterations/attempts, routing/catalogue reads and challenger
variants are zero. Keep the incumbent; a later target judgment needs separately
agreed prospective funding with k4's fixed frame/reference/history. Full variant
reads, replacement-runtime design, migration and live exposure are outside this
correctness phase. No public CLI test switches or unbounded syscall abstraction
are funded by the agreed private failure controls.

Record charged D/RC, checks still owed and remaining phase caps at closure;
carry cumulative charges and the next allocation into the root brief and next
mandate. A new leaf does not reset totals. At a bound with necessary work still
unfinished, preserve the coherent contract and precise remainder for renewed
human scope/funding rather than silently enlarging the work. Before retirement,
reconcile durable contracts only where this design establishes a current
behavioural agreement; work orders remain in the tree.
