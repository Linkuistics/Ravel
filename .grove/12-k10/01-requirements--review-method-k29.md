# review-method-k29

## Goal

Settle with the owner the method of the corpus review, just before the review
starts. The central question is the behavioural comparison that issue 6
requires: original, simplified and no-guidance controls on representative
tasks.

## Context

- Issue 6, step 7 and the acceptance criterion on representative skills.
- The running log of `plan-k1`, Topic 7.
- The 29 skills of the four packs, now in the one form after `pack-form-k28`.

## Done when

The running log records the owner's answer to each question below.

- Which skills are representative for the behavioural comparison, and which
  get a review of their text alone?
- What are the representative tasks, which harnesses run them, and how is a
  result judged?
- What counts as a substantive change of policy that the owner decides?
- Where do the original material and its provenance stay available?
- How do the readability edits follow the writing rules?

## Notes

- Bring a recommendation and its evidence to each question. The cost of the
  comparisons in harness runs is part of the evidence.
- If three or more answers depend on each other, use the full grilling
  procedure.
