## Goal
Derive the workflow inventory from the complete current reusable task list and
scripts, mapping each workflow to explanatory book text.



## Context
Use `docs/book/contract.md` and the parent brief. Run the listed development tasks
through mise. Campaigns are named evidence, not reusable or native qualification.

## Done when
Every reusable task is accounted for from the actual list and source, including
setup, checks/controls, install, release, publication and recovery. Record exact
source/book locations, digests, method and limits in durable evidence. Correct
gaps and rerun affected checks. Do not perform a release or paid harness call
merely to inventory it. Final whole-book proof and owner Read 2 remain live.
