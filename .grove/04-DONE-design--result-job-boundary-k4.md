### Local contract R-W — whole Ravel replacement

TypeScript, CLI and session promises. No new language, package count or delivery

**Result/hidden decisions.** New owners enforce admission, TypeScript/module

**Four bounds.** Oracle: existing public CLI, worker hostile-runtime and installed
