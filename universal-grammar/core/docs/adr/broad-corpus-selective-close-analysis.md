# Broad corpus with selective close analysis

URG processes a broad corpus to reveal recurring patterns and their variation. Close analysis of selected cases improves the representation and examines ambiguous, unusual or poorly captured cases. Every stage therefore runs per source and records failures, so one unavailable or malformed book does not stop a batch.

## Trade-off

Breadth shows variation that a small sample hides, but each broad analysis is shallower and less reviewed. Close analysis adds depth where breadth shows doubt.

## Rejected alternative

A small, exhaustively annotated corpus gives dense annotation. It cannot show which patterns recur across authors, so the human rejected it.
