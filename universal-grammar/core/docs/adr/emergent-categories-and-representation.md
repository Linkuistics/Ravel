# Emergent categories and representation

Categories, annotation vocabulary and formal representation are provisional, and the corpus helps reveal them. Processing books does not wait for a theory of explanation. URG keeps every observation that fits no current category.

A representation enters only when observed cases need it. Candidates are trees for document hierarchy, graphs for relationships, attributes for bindings and context, and refinement rules for recurring development. Each adopted form names the cases that justify it, and URG records which decisions stay provisional.

## Trade-off

Emergent categories fit the material, but early results have no stable scheme to compare against. Stability comes later, from patterns that recur.

## Rejected alternative

A predefined catalogue of rhetorical moves with a fixed formalism, such as a universal DSL or a graph platform, chosen before inspecting examples. It would force passages into categories and lose the observations that fit badly.
