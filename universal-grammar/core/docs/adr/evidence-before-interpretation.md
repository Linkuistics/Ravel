# Evidence before interpretation

URG stores evidence, interpretations and derived results as separate layers. Evidence does not change after extraction. It includes source passages, locations, context, document structure, links to code and figures, and bibliographic identity.

Each interpretation cites its evidence, names its author and records its uncertainty. Alternative readings coexist. A correction creates a new interpretation version that names its target and its rationale, so history stays intact and unchanged PDFs need no new extraction.

Grammars and pattern summaries derive from observations and never replace them. Each pattern names its supporting instances, its exceptions and its unresolved cases. Proposed interpretations stay distinguishable from reviewed or corrected ones.

## Trade-off

Separate layers cost storage and bookkeeping. In return, every claim stays traceable to its passages, and a correction regenerates only its dependants.

## Rejected alternative

Annotating the extracted text in place, or keeping only the current interpretation. Either approach loses the trail from a pattern to its passages, and a correction would overwrite what it corrects.
